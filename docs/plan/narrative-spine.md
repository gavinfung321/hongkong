# Narrative Spine and Scene 02 Prototype

**Status:** planned 2026-10-04. The current short copy remains the live
baseline until the scene 02 prototype is approved.

## Direction

Keep the site primarily a cinematic 3D harbour crossing. Add a restrained
editorial layer so the journey carries memory, history and emotion instead of
reading only as a sequence of attractive views.

Target balance:

- about 70% continuous 3D experience;
- about 30% text and selective illustration;
- no imitation of Kage's exact layout, artwork or weather effects.

## Narrative arc

| Scene | Story role | Central idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Departure | Dusk gathers and the crossing begins |
| 02 | Memory | The Clock Tower remembers the former railway shore |
| 03 | Ritual | The ferry makes an ordinary journey shared across generations |
| 04 | Symbol | Red sails carry Hong Kong's remembered maritime image |
| 05 | Arrival | Central rises as the modern city reached across the water |
| 06 | Afterimage | Fireworks fade while the harbour remains |

## Content format

Each chapter may eventually contain 45–75 words, divided into two short beats:

1. **Observation:** a concrete detail in the present scene.
2. **Meaning or movement:** a historical, human or emotional idea that carries
   the viewer toward the next chapter.

Keep the existing kicker and title. Never show a large paragraph all at once.
The voice remains quiet, specific and close to the water, not promotional or
academic.

All historical claims must be verified with primary or authoritative sources.
Do not invent personal memories or imply that a modern tourist junk is an
unchanged historic working vessel.

## Phase 1 — Write the journey before changing the interface

- Draft the two-beat copy for all six chapters so the complete arc can be read
  as one continuous story.
- Keep each chapter within 45–75 words.
- Remove repeated descriptions of things the 3D image already shows.
- Give every chapter a distinct purpose from the table above.
- Review the six drafts as text only. Do not edit the website yet.

### Gate

Proceed only if the six chapters read as one journey and each chapter adds
meaning that the current image cannot communicate by itself.

## Phase 2 — Build scene 02 only

Scene 02 is the prototype because the Clock Tower has strong history, a clear
landmark and enough visual space for a richer narrative.

### Text behaviour

- Keep the existing scene number, kicker and title.
- Replace the single body paragraph with two short semantic text blocks.
- Reveal the first beat with the settled Clock Tower composition.
- Reveal the second beat later in the same chapter, without hiding the first
  so abruptly that the text feels like a slideshow.
- Keep the Clock Tower readable and the 3D scene visible throughout.
- Extend the chapter scroll distance only if the two beats cannot be read at
  the current pace. Do not change the camera path merely to create reading
  time.

### Historical-memory illustration

- Create one polished editorial illustration inspired by the scene 02
  storyboard or verified railway history.
- Do not display the rough production storyboard itself.
- Match the site's coral, cream, violet and ink palette.
- Use a transparent or softly masked edge, not a rectangular picture card.
- Treat it as a brief memory layer, not the main subject.
- Keep it clear of the title, body copy and Clock Tower face.
- Use a simpler crop or omit it on mobile if the composition becomes crowded.
- Record its source, generation method and rights in `docs/ASSET-LEDGER.md` if
  it is kept.

### 3D background

- Keep the existing camera, Clock Tower, palms, water and atmospheric motion.
- Let the scene continue moving gently behind the narrative.
- Do not add rain, flicker, extra mist, new lights or another foreground effect
  during this prototype.
- Reduced motion must present a composed still background and readable text.

## Phase 3 — Decide before rolling out

Use minimal review:

- inspect scene 02 once on desktop and once on mobile in the browser;
- save one comparison image only if it helps the decision;
- run one production build and a quick console check.

Do not create a full chapter capture set, contact sheet or long recording.

Keep the prototype only if:

- scene 02 feels more meaningful and memorable;
- the Clock Tower and 3D world still lead visually;
- the two text beats are comfortable to read while scrolling;
- the illustration feels integrated rather than pasted on;
- mobile remains calm and uncluttered;
- the improvement is obvious without explanation.

If the result is weaker or merely busier, remove the prototype and retain the
current short-copy 3D showcase.

## Phase 4 — Controlled rollout

Only after scene 02 is approved:

- implement the approved two-beat copy one chapter at a time;
- review the narrative transition before moving to the next chapter;
- keep the same underlying 3D journey;
- use no more than two or three historical-memory illustrations across the
  entire site;
- do not assume every chapter needs an illustration;
- finish with one desktop and one mobile journey review.

Suggested illustration priority after scene 02:

1. scene 03, a human-scale ferry memory;
2. scene 04, a restrained maritime image only if it adds truth and context;
3. no additional illustration unless a specific chapter still feels empty.

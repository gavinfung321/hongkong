# Frame 05 — City of Light — Rough prompt

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using Frames 3–4 and the
supplied IFC and Hong Kong Observation Wheel photographs as references.

## Final generation direction

After the junk chapter, the camera leaves the vessels behind, rises from the
harbour, and approaches Central. IFC becomes the architectural hero, with the
Observation Wheel supporting it at the waterfront.

Use a wide cinematic 16:10 landscape and move the camera approximately 40–50%
closer than the first draft. Place IFC in the right half, extending from the
waterfront to its fully visible illuminated crown and occupying roughly
105–115% of the frame height. Place the Observation Wheel prominently in the
lower-left/centre-left with a diameter around 38–45% of the image height,
making it a visual co-star rather than a distant secondary detail.

Arrange supporting skyline masses so they step upward toward IFC rather than
competing with it. Retain a dark mountain silhouette and humid haze behind the
city. Retain a limited calm, dark area in the upper-left for HTML copy without
reducing the wheel's scale. A thin band of harbour water runs along the bottom,
with long amber, coral, and cyan
reflections leading toward the city. Use only restrained searchlight beams.

Do not include the junk, Star Ferry, Clock Tower, or any other vessel.

The hierarchy is:

1. IFC
2. Hong Kong Observation Wheel
3. Waterfront reflections and rising skyline
4. Supporting city and mountain haze

Render as original illustrated cinematic matte painting and editorial concept
art, with recognizable silhouettes and simplified detail. Use deep navy and
aubergine shadows, warm amber windows, a bone-white IFC crown, restrained coral
waterfront accents, sparing cyan edges, humid haze, controlled bloom, grain,
and vignette. The chapter is slightly brighter than Frames 1–4 but retains large
dark areas.

Avoid boats, sails, the Clock Tower, a cropped IFC crown, an oversized or centred
wheel, multiple wheels, equally bright surrounding skyscrapers, rainbow skyline
lighting, excessive searchlights, fake lettering, logos, signage, watermarks,
UI, Japanese elements, a moon, cyberpunk neon overload, glassmorphism, and
photorealistic travel-ad styling.

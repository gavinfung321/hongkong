## Approved creative direction candidate

Frame 3 commits fully to the ferry. The camera tracks very close alongside and
slightly behind it at wave height as it travels right from Tsim Sha Tsui toward
Central.

Use a wide cinematic 16:10 landscape. Place the ferry across most of the
lower-left half, occupying roughly 50–60 percent of the frame width. Allow its
rear-most lower-left portion or wake to approach the edge for immediacy, while
keeping enough of the double-deck form visible. Its bow points right into open
water, and its foaming wake trails toward the lower-left.

Remove the Clock Tower completely; its chapter is finished. Remove the junk
boat and all red sails completely so Frame 4 retains a clean reveal. Do not add
other vessels.

Make IFC larger and clearer in the right background as the Central destination.
Place a larger but still secondary Observation Wheel low and left/below IFC on
the waterfront. Simplify the remaining skyline with haze so the ferry, IFC, and
wheel form the only strong hierarchy. Preserve a calm dark copy-safe region in
the upper-left or upper-middle sky.

The hierarchy is:

1. Close Star Ferry
2. Directional wake and water
3. IFC destination
4. Observation Wheel
5. Supporting hazy skyline

Preserve the original illustrated cinematic matte-painting treatment, deep navy
and aubergine night, warm amber ferry windows, coral reflections, restrained
cyan rim light, humid haze, controlled bloom, grain, and vignette.

Avoid a small or static ferry, leftward travel, a visible Clock Tower, junk boat,
red sails, other vessels, oversized wheel relative to IFC, fake lettering,
logos, signage, watermarks, UI, Japanese elements, a moon, cyberpunk neon
overload, rainbow colour, and photorealistic travel-ad styling.

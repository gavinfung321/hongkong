## Final generation direction

The camera descends from Mobile Frame 1's open sky and advances toward the
Clock Tower, making the heritage departure point the sole subject.

- Centre the Clock Tower on the visual middle axis, with a modest low-angle
  view and the full crown visible.
- Let the tower occupy roughly 60–70% of the frame height.
- Use the pier, railing, palms, and warm lamps to frame the lower third.
- Limit open harbour water to a narrow 12–18% strip along the bottom.
- Keep the distant skyline subdued and atmospheric rather than panoramic.
- Preserve a quiet dark copy-safe sky area in the upper-right and a small open
  visual lane on the right for the ferry reveal in Frame 3.
- Do not show a recognizable vessel. At most, a faint far-right wake or warm
  reflection can hint at movement toward the next chapter.

Preserve the cinematic matte-painting treatment, deep navy and aubergine sky,
warm amber Clock Tower, humid haze, controlled bloom, and grain.

Avoid the Star Ferry, junk boat, red sails, IFC, Observation Wheel, fireworks,
moon, searchlights, crowds, text, logos, signage, UI, borders, and watermarks.

# Mobile Frame 01 — Harbour at Dusk — Rough prompt

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the approved desktop
Frame 1 as the visual reference. This is a purpose-built portrait composition,
not a crop of the desktop artwork.

## Generation direction

Create a very tall smartphone portrait composition matching the 390 × 844
reference viewport, with a clear diagonal landmark triangle:

- Keep the Clock Tower fully recognisable at the lower-left edge with its crown
  visible.
- Place one red-sailed junk below centre in the lower third, angled gently right
  with open water around it.
- Place IFC high on the right with its illuminated crown visible.
- Keep the Hong Kong Observation Wheel small but readable near IFC's base.
- Simplify the supporting skyline into a restrained middle band.
- Reserve a large calm, dark area in the upper-centre and upper-left for the
  opening title.
- Retain a modest water band with warm amber and restrained coral reflections.

Preserve the approved cinematic matte-painting treatment, deep navy and
aubergine atmosphere, warm architecture, coral-red sails, restrained cyan,
humid haze, controlled bloom, and grain.

Remove the Star Ferry and all other boats. Avoid fireworks, moon, prominent
searchlights, crowds, text, logos, signage, UI, borders, and watermarks.

## Mobile purpose

The portrait framing prioritises immediate landmark recognition and a generous
HTML copy-safe sky while reducing the desktop scene's secondary detail and
lateral parallax.

# Frame 02 — The Kowloon Edge — Rough prompt

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the earlier Frame 2,

## Focused creative direction

Frame 2 has a strict two-subject hierarchy: the close Clock Tower is the hero,
and a larger departing Star Ferry introduces the next chapter. IFC and the Hong
Kong Observation Wheel do not appear.

Use a wide cinematic 16:10 composition. Keep the Clock Tower fully visible from
promenade base to crown in the left 35–45 percent, occupying roughly 80–90
percent of the frame height. Retain wet stone, railing, lamps, and a few tiny
human silhouettes for scale.

Place the Star Ferry in the right-middle water, large enough to recognize but
secondary to the tower. Its bow and motion point right, away from Tsim Sha Tsui,
with a modest wake trailing left. Preserve open dark water and sky around its
route.

Replace IFC, the Observation Wheel, and other destination landmarks with a low,
soft generic harbour skyline and mountain haze. Reduce window detail,
brightness, searchlights, and landmark contrast. Do not include a junk boat or
red sails.

The hierarchy is:

1. Clock Tower
2. Departing Star Ferry
3. Wet promenade and human scale
4. Subdued harbour atmosphere

Preserve the illustrated cinematic matte-painting style, navy and aubergine
night, warm amber tower and ferry light, restrained cyan edges, wet reflections,
humid mist, controlled bloom, grain, vignette, and a dark upper-right copy-safe
region.

Avoid IFC, the Observation Wheel, junk boats, red sails, a bright detailed
skyline, leftward ferry travel, multiple ferries, distorted clock faces, fantasy
architecture, pink tower lighting, excessive palms, text, logos, signage,
watermarks, UI, Japanese elements, a moon, cyberpunk neon overload, rainbow
colour, and photorealistic travel-ad styling.

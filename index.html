01

Arrival

Harbour at Dusk

The light drains from the sky over Victoria Harbour. Across the water, the island begins to glow. Scroll to cross the harbour by night.

02

Clock Tower

The Kowloon Edge

At Tsim Sha Tsui, the old Clock Tower still marks the point of departure. A ferry pulls away from the pier and heads out into the dark water.

03

Star Ferry

Across the Water

Down at wave height, the Star Ferry carries its lit windows toward Central. Spray, engine hum, and the island growing larger with every minute.

04

The Junk

Red Sails

A junk slides alongside, its red battened sails catching the city light. For a moment the harbour slows to the pace of the wind.

05

IFC

City of Light

The island rises from the waterfront. IFC stands over Central, and the Observation Wheel turns slowly at its feet.

06

Departure

Afterglow

The last fireworks fade into smoke above the city. The crossing ends, and the harbour carries on without us.

Victoria Harbour

Harbour at Dusk

Night gathers on the water. Across the harbour, windows kindle beneath the Peak, and the night crossing begins.

02

Clock Tower

鐘樓

The Kowloon Edge

The clock still faces the harbour. The tower rose in 1915 beside the new Kowloon terminus, but its clocks and bell only began keeping time in 1921.

For sixty years, trains left from this shore for the journey north. In 1978 the station was demolished and the tower alone was kept, still standing where those journeys began.
